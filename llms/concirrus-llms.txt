# Concirrus

> Concirrus Ltd is a London-headquartered insurtech (founded 2012) selling AI-driven underwriting and submission-automation software to specialty and commercial insurers, MGAs and brokers — marine (hull, cargo, P&I), aviation, construction, property, and political violence & terrorism. Concirrus is a software vendor to the insurance market, not an insurer. As of 2026-07-25 it publishes no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/Swagger, no Postman collection, no GraphQL endpoint and no webhook/event catalog: integration is partner-gated and obtained through a commercial engagement.

## What Concirrus sells

- [Enterprise Model](https://concirrus.ai/enterprise-model-insurance-platform/): universal submission-ingestion layer covering all specialty lines, positioned for rapid onboarding
- [Underwriting Workbench](https://concirrus.ai/underwriting-workbench/): submission triage, appetite matching and real-time risk rating — marketing states Excel raters can be replicated "via API"
- [Exposure Management](https://concirrus.ai/exposure-management/): portfolio accumulation and exposure decisioning
- [Loss Run Intelligence](https://concirrus.ai/loss-run-intelligence/): structuring and reading loss-run documents
- Lines of business: [Marine](https://concirrus.ai/marine/), [Aviation](https://concirrus.ai/aviation/), [Construction](https://concirrus.ai/construction/), [Property](https://concirrus.ai/property/), [Political Violence & Terrorism](https://concirrus.ai/political-violence-terrorism/)
